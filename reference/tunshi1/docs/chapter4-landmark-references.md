## 2026-10-02 visual rebuild: completed street photographs

The new build additionally follows the eight inspected eye-level and architect photographs in [the visual-reference dossier](chapter4-visual-reference-dossier.md). These resolve continuous 4–6-storey Huangxing frontage, ordinary car lanes and tree-lined Jiefang pavements, low Taiping roofs, Pozi's red/teal Fire Palace street facade, and the completed 2025 UNIQLO silver perforated cladding and warm three-floor displays. The Fire Palace addition is an original compressed street facade, not a replica of its private interior or the older courtyard gate. Reference photos are not shipped as textures.
