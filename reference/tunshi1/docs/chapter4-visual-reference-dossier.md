# Chapter IV: downtown Changsha architectural reference

Research date: 2026-10-02. Every shortlisted image below was downloaded and opened for visual inspection. Photographs are reference material for modeled geometry and original materials; do not insert these photos into the game as texture maps.

The strongest change to the outdoor scene is continuous commercial frontage at human height. In the Huangxing photographs, shops are the ground and middle layers of adjoining buildings: glass doors, shallow steps, many facade materials, second-floor windows, sign bands, and projecting vertical signs. The sense of place comes from this accumulated frontage and a clear pedestrian corridor. It should read as a city shopping street even with the signs switched off.

- Observed: eye-level corridor with nearly continuous 4–6-storey commercial walls. Pale beige stone and dark glazed shopfronts; double-height display glass; white horizontal shop names; stacked gold/red vertical signs. Large grey rectangular stone paving continues across the pedestrian area. The central advertising kiosk is an occasional obstruction, not a whole street of obstacles. Signs and warm store interiors illuminate the paving while the high sky stays dark.

### 4. Jiefang West Road sidewalk at night

- Inspected reference filename (research copy, not a game asset): `24-jiefang-night-20221030.jpg`
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jiefangxi_Road,_Changsha.jpg
- Direct image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/41/Jiefangxi_Road%2C_Changsha.jpg
- Taken **2022-10-30**, EditQ, own photograph, CC BY-SA 4.0. Older nightlife reference.
- Observed: pedestrian sidewalk next to a moving-car avenue. Grey elongated paving joints, a yellow tactile strip, curb railings, densely parked scooters, and continuous street trees. Store doors sit above one to three shallow steps or in recessed openings. Bright white, green, cyan, red, and amber signs dominate a dark building wall; many signs are vertical or projecting. Regular amber street lamps provide a separate overhead light layer. No claim that the specific 2022 businesses still operate.

## Dimensions, distinction, and modeling guidance

| District / object | Evidence and practical shape |
|---|---|
| Huangxing South pedestrian street | Official Hunan housing page reports 838 m length, 23–26 m street width, and a nearly 10,000 m² Huangxing Square. A representative scene can anchor the corridor at 24 m. These are published street dimensions, not a current surveyed footprint. |
| Huangxing facades | Photos suggest adjoining 4–6-storey commercial frontage, with individual shop bays and lower facade detail. Modeling estimates: 3.8–4.5 m ground floor; 3.2–3.8 m upper floors; vary whole facade widths rather than giving every storefront its own freestanding building. These numeric floor heights are reconstruction assumptions. |
| Huangxing Square vs Huangxing statue | The station name is visible in reference 2. The Huangxing bronze statue is at the **north entrance** of the pedestrian street. Do not collapse the statue and station forecourt into one invented plaza. Inspected supplementary statue image shows a standing suited bronze figure with a coat over the right arm; its glass/stone base is distinct from the metro canopy. |
| Taiping | Municipal name/history reporting gives 375 m street length and stone paving. Old-street walls have small dark tiles, pitched roofs, white ridges, fire walls, and timber doors/windows. Keep the lower scale and narrow enclosure. A 6–8 m representative lane is a game composition assumption; it is not verified by these photos. |
| Pozi | Mixed heights and mixed eras. Give the Fire Palace red wall, teal eaves and gold accents a local focal role; continue ordinary beige/grey shop and residential frontage elsewhere. |
| Jiefang West | Cars, curb/railings, grey sidewalk, scooters and street trees separate it from the pedestrian corridors. A 3–5 m representative sidewalk is a modeling assumption. Shop entry recesses and shallow steps create believable detail at the player camera. |
| Nanmenkou | The selected recent image verifies the station canopy and road-edge character. Street-food alleys need separate local evidence before making claims about exact geometry. |
| Pinghetang / Wuyi Square | Recreate the broad three-level UNIQLO display facade and rounded corner from 2025, framed by larger older urban buildings. The huge sign is one landmark accent; material and glazing proportions carry the building. |
| IFS | Benoy and Wong Tung give tower heights of 452 / 315 m and a six-storey retail podium. Benoy describes more than 700 m of retail frontage. Preserve the unequal tower silhouette and the lower articulated retail mass. The current official IFS site uses 310 m for T2, so treat 315 m as the architect's published design height. |

Suggested material palette is an artistic interpretation of the inspected photos, not sampled construction specifications: Huangxing greige stone, charcoal glass, grey paving, white/red/gold/green shop accents; Taiping chalk white, weathered grey brick, dark timber and roof tile, red/gold lantern accents; Pozi terracotta red, teal roof edges, gold trims, ordinary beige residential walls; IFS blue-grey glass, charcoal mullions, pale stone and silver-white panels. Light the walls and ground with modest warm shop spill, then use small areas of saturated sign light. Night photographs and white balance do not justify making every surface emissive.

## Geometry cautions

- Storey counts, setbacks, shop widths, and camera eye level above are visual estimates unless explicitly tied to a published dimension. Do not present them as an exact 1:1 reconstruction.
- Telephoto photographs compress lane depth; crowds hide pavement and door thresholds. The eye-level broad corridor and direct facade photograph are better proportion references than tightly cropped crowd shots.
- The named Commons "Changsha Pedestrian Street" series includes the car intersection opposite IFS; its category title alone does not prove a pedestrian-only location.
- IFS architect pages contain old completion wording; the 2019 gallery is form evidence, not a 2026 construction report.
- Shop tenants and giant screen advertisements change. Keep the dated architecture, and create original procedural signs/materials suitable for the game.
